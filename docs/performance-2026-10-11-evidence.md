## Limits

- Whole-frame GPU time is not available: the Vulkan terrain backend publishes per-pass timestamp-query times, the pinned OpenGL reference publishes none, and nothing here measures the swap/present GPU cost of either configuration. 'GPU time' in this document therefore means the renderer's own per-terrain-pass timestamps only.
- The CPU time is the whole process (render thread, client thread, integrated-server thread and GC together), not the render thread alone.
- The headless compositor's output is 1280x720@60Hz; the achieved frame times, not the vsync flag alone, are the evidence about whether either cap bound the run.
- This machine is shared with other agents. Every run records the load average at both ends of its measured window and the external sampler's band; runs outside the load gate are marked and excluded from the deltas, and the write-up states whether the compared runs were balanced.
- The chunk phase measures the frame times of a fixed post-placement window and, where the renderer publishes queue counters, the frame at which the queue first read zero. It is the cost of the same 64 block placements in the same flat world, not a general chunk-generation benchmark.
- The pinned upstream jar is one commit (989ebf2e) of one project built from the repository's own baseline workspace; nothing here says anything about other versions.
